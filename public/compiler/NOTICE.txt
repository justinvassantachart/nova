web-ide standard-library precompiled headers

These assets are generated from the unchanged headers in the debugger-sh
C++ sysroot, SHA-256:
8745c6af23fd841fa507d0c73f6b6b8e8da1b4ec216a6339739a28b38ff7227b
https://fabioibanez.github.io/website/llvm-resources.tar.gz

The generated files are gzip-compressed PCH data (.pch.bin). They are built
with the exact compiler and options recorded in manifest.json. The source
recipe is tools/compiler/build-pch.mjs in the nova repository. No Stanford
library source or course PCH is included in these files.
