web-ide standard-library precompiled headers

The legacy assets in this directory are generated from the unchanged headers in the debugger-sh
C++ sysroot, SHA-256:
8745c6af23fd841fa507d0c73f6b6b8e8da1b4ec216a6339739a28b38ff7227b
https://fabioibanez.github.io/website/llvm-resources.tar.gz

The generated files are gzip-compressed PCH data (.pch.bin). They are built
with the exact compiler and options recorded in manifest.json. The source
recipe is tools/compiler/build-pch.mjs in the nova repository. No Stanford
library source or course PCH is included in these files.

LLVM libc++ headers: Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception.
LLVM compiler/sysroot family: YoWASP llvm-project
9560ae0f2cc440e4fc891fddbc119da6f56daa59 (LLVM 22.1.0).
https://codeberg.org/YoWASP/llvm-project
See licenses/LLVM-LICENSE.txt.

WASI libc headers retain the licenses documented in
licenses/wasi-libc-LICENSE.txt and its accompanying license/copyright files.
Their upstream source is https://github.com/WebAssembly/wasi-libc,
revision 2fc32bc (the sysroot provenance recorded by the original project).

The engine fork includes its own exception runtime. Its unmodified notices,
licenses and build provenance are served under
/third-party/debugger-sh/share/licenses/cpp-exceptions/.

The separately generated upstream/ assets use unchanged libc++ headers from
the sysroot with SHA-256:
c549ba2c715645298ddec4609859b6982188ab4662326ad023a4568a289f3a7b
Their LLVM source revision is 392dfff97d29b59d77e5cac6d4b6ef2585fe7117
from https://github.com/llvm/llvm-project (Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception).
The debugger-sh LLVM release and checksum-addressed compiler/sysroot URLs,
complete build flags, and required runtime tuple are recorded in
upstream/manifest.json. The upstream exception libraries are included in the
verified sysroot, not supplied by the legacy exception overlay.
